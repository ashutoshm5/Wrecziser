                    WRECZISER - CHROME EXTENSION SETUP
================================================================================

Congratulations! Wrecziser desktop application has been installed successfully.

Follow these 3 quick steps to enable the Wrecziser Chrome Extension:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: Open Chrome Extensions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. In the URL address bar, type or paste:
   chrome://extensions
   and press ENTER.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: Enable Developer Mode
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Look at the top-right corner of the Extensions page.
2. Turn ON the toggle switch labeled: "Developer mode".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: Load the Wrecziser Extension
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the top-left corner, click the button labeled:
   "Load unpacked"

2. When the folder picker opens, copy and paste this exact folder path into
   the address bar at the top of the picker, then press ENTER:

   C:\Users\shiv1\AppData\Local\Programs\Wrecziser

3. Click "Select Folder".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALL DONE!
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Wrecziser icon will now appear in your Chrome toolbar.
Pin it to your toolbar for instant 1-click summaries, LinkedIn job extraction,
and resume targeting.
